Birds

Eagle

bird of prey belonging to the family Accipitridae. It is one of the most powerful and majestic raptors.

Key Characteristics:

Size: Eagles range in length from about 60 cm to over 1 meter. Some species, like the Philippine eagle, can have a wingspan of up to 2.5 meters. Sharp Vision: Eagles have incredibly sharp eyesight, 4-8 times stronger than human vision. This allows them to spot prey from great distances (up to 3 kilometers away).

Penguins

Penguins are amazing birds that cannot fly. Instead, they use their wings as flippers to swim expertly in the ocean. Most penguins live in the Southern Hemisphere, especially in cold places like Antarctica. They have black and white feathers that act as camouflage, helping them stay safe from predators. Even though they look clumsy when they waddle on land, they are incredibly fast and graceful underwater.

The parrot is one of the most intelligent and colorful birds in the world. Known for its ability to mimic human speech and other sounds, the parrot is a popular pet and a fascinating creature in the wild. Parrots are found mainly in tropical and subtropical regions, such as South America, Australia, and Southeast Asia. They have strong, curved beaks and zygodactyl feet (two toes facing forward and two backward), which help them climb and hold onto branches.
