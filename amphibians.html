Amphibians

Amphibians are cold-blooded animals that can live both in water and on land. They usually start their life in water as eggs and larvae, then change into adults that can survive on land. They have moist skin that helps them breathe and are found in wet environments like ponds, rivers, and forests.

FROG

frog have smooth,moist skin that helps them breath,and they lives near water and feeds on insects,they also has strong back legs that help them jump and swim. the sound they make is called "croaking" they use it to communicate with eachother.

AXOLOTL

axolotl eat small animals like worms,insects and tiny fish.and they can regenerate their limbs. and lives in fresh water lakes and canals mainly in mexico,and they are often called "walking fish"but they are not fishes.

SALAMANDER

salamanders have long bodies,short legs,and tails most of them live in moist places like forests,near streams,or under rocks because their skin is soft and absorbs water.they are mostly active at night and hide during the day.
